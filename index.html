Our Heritage

Where Devotion
Becomes Art

Himalayan Thangka Art Gallery was founded in the ancient alleys of Kathmandu with a single purpose: to bring the living tradition of Himalayan sacred painting to collectors and seekers worldwide.

Every piece in our collection is hand-painted using centuries-old techniques — natural mineral pigments, pure gold leaf, and hand-woven cotton canvas — by artists who carry lineages of devotional craft.

We believe art made in meditation carries a different energy. Each thangka is more than an object — it is a window into awakened mind.

The Sacred Craft

How a Thangka is Born

A single painting can take months of meditative work

01

Canvas Preparation

Cotton cloth is stretched, coated with chalk and hide glue, then polished to a smooth ivory surface.

02

Sacred Geometry

The composition is laid out using traditional grid systems — proportions passed down through generations.

03

Mineral Pigments

Natural stones — lapis lazuli, malachite, cinnabar — are ground and bound with ox-bile to create vivid, lasting colors.

04

Gold & Consecration

Pure gold leaf is applied with a burnishing stone. The finished work is blessed in a ritual ceremony.
